The certainty of meeting your customer in person, delivered digitally. No passwords. No SMS OTPs. No MFA tools. Everything dynamically linked by design.

Recreating the certainty of meeting in person

In a branch, you know who is in front of you.

You see the person, you check the document they hold, and you watch them sign. That is certainty: identity, presence and intent confirmed together, in one moment.

Digital channels broke that moment into fragments. A password confirms a secret, an SMS code confirms a SIM, an MFA prompt confirms an app, and a device biometric confirms an unlocked phone.

Each tool works in its own silo. None can validate the others, none can confirm the person, and stacked together they still cannot replicate the certainty of meeting face to face.

One glance replaces them all

No passwords. No SMS OTPs. No MFA tools.

The person, the device and the action are proven together and dynamically linked by design. The certainty of a branch visit, in every digital interaction.

Nothing to remember

No passwords or PINs to create, reset or steal.

Nothing to intercept

No SMS codes to redirect through SIM swaps or phishing.

Nothing extra to install

No separate MFA apps or hardware tokens. Multi-factor by design.

Who is there, and what they approved.

Two questions every institution must answer with proof. One action answers both.

Authentication

Proving who is there

  • Passwordless loginA single glance authenticates both the person and their device.
  • True multi-factorInherence and possession, each verified independently, in one action.
  • Step-upHigher assurance for sensitive actions, with the same single glance.
  • Account recoverySelf-service recovery with the customer's face. No helpdesk, no SMS codes.
  • EnrollmentA biometric scan certified by global standards, or enrollment from your existing KYC.

Authorization

Proving what they approved

  • Dynamic linkingEvery approval is cryptographically tied to the exact amount and payee, or the specific action.
  • Transaction signingA unique signature per transaction, bound to the person and their device.
  • Digital consentConsent that cannot be denied later, with an auditable record of who approved what.
  • Digital signaturesDocuments signed with a verified identity, not just a click.

When a customer disputes a transaction, the institution must prove it was authenticated.

Here, that proof is built into every action: who approved it, on which device, for exactly what. See the regulatory shift
