The shift

Citizens prove who they are again and again, yet no tool proves the person.

Too many tools, no single proof

Passwords, SMS codes, tokens and document uploads each establish a fragment of identity or device possession. Every tool is another silo to buy, secure and support, and none confirms the person.

Fraud is harder to see

Deepfakes and synthetic identities target remote onboarding and digital services. When a citizen disputes a signature or a consent, the authority must be able to prove who acted.

Data protection is a duty

Every repeated upload and central database widens exposure. Citizens should share only what each service needs, and nothing more.
