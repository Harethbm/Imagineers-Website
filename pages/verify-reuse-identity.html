The shift

Identity is moving from central databases to the people it describes.

Verification, repeated everywhere

The same person is verified again by every bank, platform and authority, each time handing personal data to a new database.

Central databases, central risk

Centralized identity stores create single points of failure, breach targets and vendor lock-in.

The world is moving

The EU now requires every Member State to offer a digital identity wallet under eIDAS 2.0, and the W3C has standardized Verifiable Credentials for portable, user-controlled identity.

Privacy by design

What the verifier learns, and what it never sees.

Learns

Only what the request needs

  • The claim is trueFor example, "over 18" or "identity verified"
  • A trusted issuer verified itA bank, operator or authority
  • The rightful holder presented itFrom their own device
  • It is current and unalteredTamper-evident by design

Never sees

Unless the holder chooses to share

  • The date of birthBehind an "over 18" proof
  • The full identity documentOnly the attributes requested
  • Any biometric dataNever stored, never exposed
  • Anything outside the requestSelective disclosure and zero-knowledge proofs
