The shift

SMS OTP is being retired. The assets that replace it are already yours.

Saturated markets

Connectivity alone no longer differentiates. Growth now depends on new services built on what operators uniquely hold.

A thin assumption

SMS OTP was meant to prove possession of a device. It only proves a message reached a number, and SIM swaps and interception break even that. NIST now classifies it as a restricted authenticator, and banks in Singapore have begun phasing it out.

Assets you cannot use yet

In most markets, regulation already requires you to verify every subscriber. Without a way to capture consent and share to global standards, that trust stays locked inside your network.
