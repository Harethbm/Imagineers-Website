The problem

Today's systems verify credentials, not people.

Authentication still rests on stand-ins. A username and password are treated as proof of who you are. An SMS code or an MFA app is treated as proof of the device you hold. Each has been routinely defeated: passwords are phished, SMS codes are intercepted or redirected through SIM swaps, and MFA prompts are pushed until someone approves.

Knowing a secret is not being someone.

Passwords, one-time codes and voice checks prove only that someone knew, received or sounded like something. Each one can be phished, intercepted or faked.

Biometrics as built today create new risk.

Identity-verification systems store face data in honeypots exposed to deepfakes, and forget the person once onboarding ends. Device biometrics fall back to a PIN, so they prove the phone was unlocked, not who unlocked it.

Passkeys prove the device, not the person.

They rely on device biometrics, so a PIN can stand in for the person, and nothing ties the key to a verified identity.

Actions happen, but nothing links them.

Without a binding proof of who approved each transaction, there is no accountability.

None of these methods can prove that the person authenticating is the same person who enrolled.

How the Unified Identity Assurance Platform closes the gap

The value

Certainty, bound to the person.

Verifiable credentials change the model. With zero-knowledge proofs, a customer proves a fact, such as “over 18” or “identity verified”, and reveals nothing beyond that fact. Every credential is digitally signed by its issuer, so any alteration is instantly detectable. Each time it is presented, it is bound to three things at once: the holder's own face, matched without storing a biometric template; a key that never leaves the holder's device; and the KYC record it was issued from.

The person, proven.

Privacy-preserving biometrics confirm that the person acting is the person who enrolled, without any biometric data being stored on the device or the server.

Every action, bound.

Each transaction is cryptographically signed and tied to the person, their device, the app, and the exact amount and payee. Consent can't be denied, and signatures stand up in disputes.

Nothing to steal.

No passwords or codes to phish, no templates to breach, and a post-quantum-ready design that leaves nothing worth harvesting.

One identity, every channel.

A single step delivers true multi-factor assurance, and self-service recovery uses the customer's face, with no call centre and no SMS codes.

Everything on premise, under your control.

Deployed in your own infrastructure, so customer data, server-side keys and audit records stay within your environment and your governance.
